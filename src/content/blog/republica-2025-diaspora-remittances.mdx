Our CEO, Seronei Chelulei Cheison, was honoured to speak at [re:publica 2025](https://re-publica.com/) in Berlin on 27 May 2025 on the theme **"From 'Black Tax' to 'Business Capital', Transforming African Diaspora Remittances"**.

The talk highlighted the power of diaspora remittances, a whopping US$3,000 per second flowing to Africa, and ten times that globally. There is an opportunity for growth.
