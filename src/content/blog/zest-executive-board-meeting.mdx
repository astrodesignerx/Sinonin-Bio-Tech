The #ZESTProject held its first executive board meeting last Monday. During this hour-long online gathering, representatives from the organisations that support the European initiative went through various specific topics that touch on governance and oversight, performance review, and risk management.

Sinonin Biotech was represented by the CEO, Seronei.

🌱 [#ZEST](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/hashtag/?keywords=zest&highlightedUpdateUrns=urn%3Ali%3Aactivity%3A7247165636022329345) is supported by the [Circular Bio-based Europe Joint Undertaking (CBE JU)](https://www.linkedin.com/company/cbe-ju/) and its members.
