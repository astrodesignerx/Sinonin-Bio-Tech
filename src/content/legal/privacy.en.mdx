## Privacy Policy

**1. Data protection at a glance**

**General notes.** The following information provides a simple overview of what happens to your personal data when you visit this website. Personal data is any data with which you could be personally identified.

**Data recording on this website.** This website does not use cookies, analytics tools or tracking technologies. When you visit the site, our hosting provider automatically collects technical server log data (IP address, date and time of the request, browser type and version, operating system, referrer URL) that is required for the secure operation of the website. This processing is based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. our legitimate interest in a secure and stable website.

**3. Contact forms and report requests**

If you send us an inquiry via a contact form or request a market report, we process the data you provide (first and last name, email address, optionally phone number or company, and your message) exclusively to handle your inquiry and, where applicable, to deliver the requested report.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give via the consent checkbox, and, where the inquiry relates to a potential contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. You may withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future by emailing us.

Form submissions are transmitted via the form service **FormSubmit** (WebDev LLC, USA), which processes the data on our behalf to deliver it to us by email. This may involve a transfer of personal data to a third country (United States). Details: [https://formsubmit.co/privacy.pdf](https://formsubmit.co/privacy.pdf).

We retain your inquiry until the purpose for which it was collected has been fulfilled and no legal retention obligations apply, after which it is deleted.

**4. Email inquiries**

If you contact us by email, we process the personal data transmitted (email address, name, content of the inquiry) to handle your request on the basis of Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract-related inquiries) or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in answering inquiries).

**5. Social media links**

Our website contains simple outbound links to our social media profiles (LinkedIn, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, X). No social media plugins are embedded; when you visit our website, no data is transmitted to these providers. If you follow a link, the respective provider's privacy policy applies.

**6. Your rights**

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

- Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR)
- Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
- Right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
- Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
- Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
- Right to object to processing (Art. 21 GDPR)
- Right to withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3) GDPR)
- Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, for us, the [Data Protection Authority of Lower Saxony](https://lfd.niedersachsen.de) (Die Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz Niedersachsen)

To exercise any of these rights, simply email us at [info@SinoninBio.tech](mailto:info@SinoninBio.tech).

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website or legal requirements. The current version is always available on this page.
